FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is the app safe?

The APK comes directly from our official GitHub release. Android may show a warning the first time because the app is installed from outside the Play Store. For how data is handled, see the Privacy page.

How do I use it on an iPhone?

The APK works on Android only. On iPhone, tap Open on web, then use Share in Safari and choose Add to Home Screen.

My institute is not in the list.

The APK for your institute may not be built yet. Please ask your institute. Until then, Open on web gives you the full app.

Where do I get the join code?

Your institute's admin or teacher will give you the join code.
