Privacy Policy — The Vishnu Sharma

Version 2026-11-24-v1 · Last updated: 24 November 2026

Draft for review: Owner should get this checked by a qualified professional before public release. Not legal advice.

Short mein: Aapka naam, mobile, class aur test results sirf padhai ke kaam aate hain. Aapka institute aapka data dekh sakta hai. Hum ads ke liye data use nahi karte aur kisi ko bechte nahi. Account aap kabhi bhi yahan se delete kar sakte hain.

What we collect

Name, mobile number, institute and class; test answers, scores, mistakes and scan photos; basic app/device details to fix errors. Passwords and PINs are stored in protected form, not as plain text.

Why we use it

To run your account, tests and results, show your progress, sync offline work, send notifications you allow, and give support. No ads. No selling of data.

Who can see it

You. Your institute's admins (only for their own institute). Us and our service providers, only to run, secure or support the app. If you join more than one institute, each account is separate and an institute sees only its own.

Where it is stored

On Google Firebase and other trusted cloud services. Data may be stored outside your state or country.

Children

If you are under 18, register only with a parent or guardian's permission. The registration form records this.

Delete and your choices

You can delete your account any time on the Delete account/data page (or in Student Settings). This removes your profile, results, scan reports, mistakes, sessions and batch records for that account. Copies already exported by an institute, or held in provider backups, may remain for a short time. Ask your institute to correct wrong details. Never share your password or PIN with anyone.

Security

We use access controls to protect your data, but no service is 100% secure. Report any problem through Help & Support in the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version and date will be shown here.
